# Credits and purchases

Rotur has its own currency, **credits**. People earn them, send them to each other, and spend them on keys, items and cosmetics.

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Transactions and taxes](transactions-and-taxes.md) | How credits move between accounts, and the fees on each kind of transfer |
| [Earn credits](earn-credits.md) | The ways people earn credits |
| [Keys](keys.md) | Sell access to your app or content, once or as a subscription |
| [Cosmetics](cosmetics/README.md) | Profile cosmetics people buy, equip and gift |

Items and gifts are part of the Claw API: see [Items](../../claw/api-endpoints/items.md) and [Gifts](../../claw/api-endpoints/gifts.md).

If your app lets people spend, also see [Declarations and safety signals](../../build-an-app/safety.md#may-they-spend): parents can limit what their teen spends, and your app must ask before each purchase.
